Stay Safe Online Checklist for College Students
General Online Safety
- Use Strong, Unique Passwords
Create different passwords for each account. Avoid using names, birthdays, or simple sequences. Consider a password manager.
- Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Always activate 2FA for email, social media, and banking apps to add an extra layer of security.
- Avoid Public Wi-Fi for Sensitive Tasks
Don’t log into banking or academic portals on unsecured public networks. Use mobile data or a trusted VPN.
- Think Before You Click
Don’t open suspicious links or attachments in emails, WhatsApp, or SMS—even if they look official.
- Keep Devices Updated
Regularly update your phone, laptop, and apps to patch security vulnerabilities.
- Limit Personal Information Sharing
Be cautious about posting your phone number, address, or financial details on social media.

Financial & UPI Safety
- Verify UPI Requests Before Paying
Double-check the recipient’s name and UPI ID before approving any payment. Fraudsters often mimic official names.
- Never Share UPI PIN or OTP
Banks and payment apps will never ask for your PIN or OTP. Treat these like confidential keys.
Reporting & Support- Report Cybercrime Immediately
If you face online fraud, harassment, or suspicious activity, report it at the National Cyber Crime Portal: cybercrime.gov.in.
- Call Helpline 1930 for Urgent Financial Fraud
Use this toll-free number to quickly report UPI or online banking scams.
